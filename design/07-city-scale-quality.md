# 07 — Paris at city scale

Engineering roadmap · 2026-11-22 · Engine measurement baseline `16bb6e5` · Paris scope reviewed at `e34f242`

**First deliverable.** A player descends from 600 m above the Seine at Pont de Bir-Hakeim. The Eiffel Tower stays visible where the sightline is clear. At the bridge, one approach reaches the upper rail deck and a second reaches the lower level from the side. The replay records which surface caught the player and when its collider became ready. Build the first 2 × 2 km around the bridge from Paris map data and run it in the independent `ville-paris` experience. [City account of the two decks](https://www.paris.fr/pages/le-pont-bir-hakeim-retrouve-une-seconde-jeunesse-cet-ete-8169) · [View of the tower from the bridge](https://www.paris.fr/pages/l-olympiste-arena-bercy-stade-tour-eiffel-par-la-rive-gauche-27870)

**Release objective.** Sustain a continuous, persistent, 1:1 world of Paris, France, within the boundary of commune 75056. Its 20 municipal arrondissements include the Bois de Boulogne and Bois de Vincennes. The city reports 105.4 km² including the Seine and both woods. Use the union of the official arrondissement polygons as the playable footprint. Streets, slopes, both riverbanks and their crossings must line up on native and browser clients. Target convincing detail on foot, responsive movement, dense crowds and traffic, and stable light in motion. Qualify the engine first against a fixed external reference, then demonstrate an advantage in a blind comparison and in measured engine behaviour. Observation supplies situations to test. Implementation inputs are Osage code, original or licensed open content, public specifications and research. Performance and quality thresholds below are proposed gates. [INSEE commune record](https://www.insee.fr/fr/metadonnees/geographie/commune/75056-paris) · [Paris figures](https://cdn.paris.fr/paris/2025/12/12/paris_ra2024-chiffres-cles-3-pages-09-12-copie-qJLj.pdf) · [Official arrondissement boundaries](https://opendata.paris.fr/explore/dataset/arrondissements/) · [Contribution rules](../CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Engine architecture](adr/0001-architecture.md)

**Playable pilot.** `worlds/ville-paris` has its own native window, geometry and replay. Its original metric scene exercises both bridge decks and a roof. The mapped pilot is still an acceptance target. S0 uses the separate Bistro world as a lighting control. [Run the experience and its checks](../knowledge/modules/ville-paris.md)

Jump to the [Paris map inputs](#paris-map-inputs), the [delivery order](#delivery-order) or the [first three pull requests](#first-three-pull-requests).

The first sector has an independent triangle-contact check and a controlled window-detail comparison. On Iris Xe at 1280 × 720 the median of four run-level p95s fell from 40.20 to 34.57 ms. One of the four pairs regressed. Four fixed pictures had mean absolute RGB error below 0.23 on an 8-bit scale. The external browser runtime rejected the supplied level name, so the matched external measurement is still open. [Measurement tools and method](../knowledge/modules/ville-paris.md)

## Paris map inputs

Q02 builds the pilot on both banks, from Grenelle to Passy. Store its boundary as a projected 2 × 2 km crop centred on the bridge midpoint in the frozen source geometry. Use the municipal arrondissement polygons for the boundary, IGN BD TOPO for building footprints, streets, water and rail, and IGN RGE ALTI for ground elevation. Use the city's street survey to check the positions of kerbs and bridge approaches where available. Record each source edition, feature ID, coordinate system, licence, attribution and file digest. Carry source IDs through to the cooked mesh and collider. A building footprint or ground sample gives insufficient information to certify a bridge deck or an interior. Model these separately, tag their provenance, and gate the two deck heights on independent elevation evidence. [IGN BD TOPO](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/bd-topo-r) · [IGN RGE ALTI](https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/rge-alti-r) · [Paris street survey](https://opendata.paris.fr/explore/dataset/pcrs-paris/)

Transform the source geometry in f64 metres into one declared Lambert-93 frame and NGF-IGN69 normal heights. Pin the horizontal realization, vertical transformation and city origin `(E0,N0,H0)` in the manifest. Convert to Osage's grid axes as `(x,y,z) = (E−E0,H−H0,N0−N)` before quantising cell-local offsets. Test conversion in both directions on independent control points on opposite banks. Check map alignment against the published source accuracy. Use a separate 0.10 m limit for numerical round-trip error and test east, north and up offsets explicitly to catch a sign swap. Publish the corresponding geodetic origin through the existing `WorldDescriptor.earth_frame`, converting the origin height to ellipsoidal height and accounting for the angle between grid north and true north at the origin. Native, browser and server must resolve a checkpoint to the same cell and support ID. [IGN on Lambert-93 realizations](https://geodesie.ign.fr/actualites/evolution-venir-des-codes-epsg-du-rgf93) · [IGN height systems](https://geodesie.ign.fr/faq) · [World descriptor](../spec/proto/osage/v0/protocol.proto) · [Paris walking axes](../worlds/paris/world/src/walk.rs)

The existing Bistro street and camera remain the S0 lighting control with their own scene ID and coordinate frame. Geographic acceptance starts at S1 on the mapped pilot. Keep surveyed geometry, reconstructed detail and authored interiors distinguishable in the asset manifest. ## Targets and evidence

| Dimension | Release gate |
|---|---|
| Frame pacing | Apply the existing tier frame budgets to **p95** on S0–S3 and S6. T1 720p ≤33.3 ms, T2 1080p ≤16.7 ms on Iris Xe, T3 1440p ≤16.7 ms and T4 1440p ≤33.3 ms on L4. Each p99 ≤1.5× its tier budget. For an initial T3 pass allocation aim at CPU main thread ≤10 ms and GPU ≤14 ms p95, measured separately because they overlap. For T4 aim at GPU ≤29 ms. Record internal and output resolution, dynamic resolution limits and effective ray features. [Existing budgets and reference machines](../bench/README.md) |
| Streaming | No blank frame or wrong support surface on any replay. On the ordinary link ≥99% of requested visible refinements reach the chosen level by their deadline. Boot readiness includes a valid camera, inputs, collidable starting area and skyline. Aim for ≤5 s p95 from join on S5 with the 2 s cut disabled, and ≤2 s on a warm cache. Report bytes to first input and delay to first fine detail separately. |
| Memory and CPU | Initial combined budget for **resident streamable content** is 512 MiB on T1, 768 MiB on T2, 1.5 GiB on T3 and 2 GiB on T4, including decoded pages, pinned parents, in-flight uploads and render copies. Repeat the route ten times and return to within 5% of the post-warm-up content footprint. |
| Simulation and network | On four dedicated reference vCPUs, 32 simulated clients plus the S3 population, 30 region ticks/s, p99 world step including two 60 Hz substeps ≤25 ms. Bound volatile replication to 128 kbit/s per client at p95 over one-second windows in this fixture. Report reliable traffic and content separately. Every accepted interaction has one authoritative outcome on every entitled client. |
| Pictures, motion and sound | Use matching fixed-camera Cycles frames and masks for the floor, near façade, sky and moving actors. For the existing S0 five-probe view, preserve ≥27.8 dB whole-frame PSNR and each regional score at least as high as its matching control minus 0.5 dB. Score radiance in linear light before the view transform and image PSNR after the same AgX transform. In motion count holes, disocclusions, silhouette displacement and ghost duration. Score audiovisual continuity with the comparative trial below. Carry the same tier gates onto rendered S7 routes. [Reference render](../tools/bistro/truth.py) · [Image measurements](../research/10-incoming-light.md) |

For a healthy online control, cap bulk content at 100 Mbit/s, set round-trip latency to 20 ms with zero injected loss, and record the actual throughput. Run 40 independent cold joins and 40 warm joins per target to estimate startup p95. The S5 fault run uses its own link profile.

Alternate control and candidate five times on the same build settings, machine and driver after one warm-up. Save each run and judge the median of the five run percentiles. Also report the worst frame and require every run to pass the correctness invariants. Test cold process/content cache separately from warm steady-state and report shader compilation time. Time presented frames with a wall clock and bounded frames in flight. Capture every frame for integrity in a separate run so image writing does not distort pacing. Count camera cuts and late frames in the report. Mark unavailable optional counters in the receipt. Missing evidence for a required gate leaves that gate inconclusive.

**Comparative trial.** At Q01 freeze the requested external title's retail build, platform, edition, preset and display conditions in the evaluator's private run sheet under reference A. Store a build fingerprint, preset digest and machine identifier in each anonymised result so a second evaluator can repeat it. Use its conventional raster preset for T3 and its enhanced lighting preset for T4 on a platform where that profile is available. Register one common supported PC, FOV, displayed resolution, controller, frame cap, route descriptions and recording method. Match observable traffic density, moving actors, illumination, travel speed and sightlines within registered bands. Record equivalent activities in each world. Use absolute image metrics only for different renders of the *same* Osage scene. A cloud timing from one application and a console timing from another cannot establish a relative frame-time result.

Before viewing results, fix camera tasks, sample size (initial proposal 60 independent participants), random order and scoring. Hide product labels, balance order, allow ties worth half a vote, and record whether participants recognised the source. The six independently rated axes are distant continuity, materials/light in motion, human and animal movement, driving/contact response, crowd and traffic behaviour, and audiovisual coherence. Give participants playable trials for the response axis. Use a participant-clustered bootstrap and simultaneous 95% intervals across the six axes and two declared comparison profiles. **Parity** requires all lower bounds for Osage preference ≥45% on both profiles and all technical gates. **Beyond parity** requires lower bounds >55% on at least two predeclared axes (distant continuity and movement), plus a ≥20% lower p99 frame time on S2’s predeclared matched wet route at equal output settings with the cap disabled for the timing run. Repeat on a second supported PC. Publish settings, counts, uncertainty and Osage receipts. A missing matched external run leaves the external verdict open. Publish the internal gates that passed.

[Object proposal](06-objects.md) · [Existing exporter](../tools/bistro/export.py) · [Content entry point](../crates/osage-content/src/lib.rs) · [3D Tiles hierarchy](https://github.com/CesiumGS/3d-tiles/blob/main/specification/README.adoc)

### Q03 — Visual levels with a known geometric error

**Content/render · after Q02.** Cook building and prop levels with attribute-aware simplification. Lock tile boundaries, hard edges and material/UV seams. Measure conservative geometric error against the source, including quantisation. Atlas coarse façades and bake a far silhouette for whole blocks. Give foliage a separate thinning/impostor path. At run time choose a cut of the object tree using projected error in pixels, with vertical FOV in radians. Start with `error_m × viewport_height / (2 × tan(fov_y/2) × nearest_depth_m)`. Clamp depth at the near plane. Subdivide above 2 px, coarsen below 1 px as initial T3 settings. A parent continues to draw until all required visible children have a drawable payload. Use a complementary dither during the transition. [Prop levels](../crates/osage-props/src/lod.rs) · [Tree cards](../crates/osage-props/src/impostor.rs) · [meshoptimizer simplification](https://meshoptimizer.org/#simplification)

**Accept** on S0 when the first coarse level saves ≥40% of encoded mesh bytes, loses ≤0.5 dB against the full ring in each façade mask and moves the 95th-percentile outline by ≤1 pixel at the declared switch distance. Test head-on, grazing and oscillating viewpoints. S1 must always have a drawable ancestor and draw each opaque branch once outside the short fade. Emit source, unique, resident, culled and submitted triangle counts independently.

### Q04 — One deadline planner for distinct kinds of data

**Streaming · after Q02.** Add a pure `plan(now, observers, bodies, inventory, costs, limits) -> demands` beside the cube selection. Wrap Paris `needed_light` as a producer. A demand carries cell/object, payload kind (collision, navigation, visual level, texture mip, incoming light), revision, earliest use, deadline, cost estimate and priority. Combine observers by the earliest deadline. Predict swept body bounds and camera cones independently. A spectator camera may raise visual priority but cannot move the simulation's collision interest. [Cube reach](../crates/osage-cubes/src/lib.rs) · [Paris priorities](../worlds/paris/world/src/tiles.rs)

Estimate lead time from per-class read, transfer, decode, queue and publication latency plus a safety margin. Measure end-to-end p99 deadline misses too. For collision use a conservative bound `speed × lead + 0.5 × max_accel × lead² + swept_radius`. With proposed sample values of 55 m/s, 0.40 s, 9.81 m/s² and 2 m, prepare about **25 m** ahead along the trajectory. This is a worked test input. Recompute it for a fast car, a new link, a hard turn or a moving landing surface. Within each priority class serve the least slack first. Reserve bytes and worker time for support surfaces and the visible coarse cut. Age optional work within its own allowance.

**Accept** in a virtual-clock test with recorded S1/S5 inputs. Requests for the landing surface must precede optional detail, duplicate observers produce one read per hash, a camera cut cancels stale visual work, and the planner performs zero I/O. Log the predicted and actual time to first use so the coefficient can be tuned from traces.

### Q05 — Read, decode and publish within bounded budgets

**Streaming/runtime · after Q03 and Q04.** Implement a bounded executor for local files and immutable HTTP chunks. Keep selection outside the worker pool. Track `queued → reading → verified → decoded → staged → resident` for each payload and generation. Track collision, mesh, material and light readiness independently. Check the declared size and digest before decoding, limit decoded size, coalesce readers of the same hash, and discard stale completions. Use a byte-limited CPU queue and a per-frame GPU upload allowance. Publish after the render fence or at a simulation tick boundary as appropriate. ## Qualification of local district pages

The original Bir-Hakeim fixture is available as a coarse visual parent, 323 detailed visual tiles and 309 physical tiles. The paging replay advances the same 60 Hz descent with actual file reads under a simulated transfer clock. At 20 Mbit/s per channel, both 200 and 600 ms request delays passed with a radius of 64 m for collision and 48 m for fine visual geometry. Holding the collision tile through impact lets the capsule pass through the bridge. Holding only the visual tile retains coarse detail at the correct physical landing. The protected replay now reads a 23,690-byte catalogue of exact upward support faces. The rail, road and roof land at ticks 807, 67 and 790 with a two-second page delay. The complete page replaces the support in the solver. Vertical walls and ceilings still require their physical page. [Method and replay](../knowledge/algorithms/district-paging.md)

The 1,000-approach run uses a fixed seed, four arrival conditions, and a reference with resident geometry for every route. Predictive contacts on the single capsule reduced the maximum downward excursion from 79.28 mm to 9.75 mm. The 4 cm limit went from 62 failures to zero. The protected runs matched the reference contact tick in all cases. In the later district format, shared visual model pages and material instances use a 96 m visual request radius with a 64 m physical radius on the three measured descents. A 96-case sweep includes separate impulse and surface-crossing controls at the internal road edge.
