# Problem

At Pont de Bir-Hakeim a capsule above the bridge can land on the railway at 16 m. A capsule already between the two decks can land on the road at 8 m. Both surfaces belong to the same streaming cell. A detached camera can also request a picture of the bridge while the actor is elsewhere.[^sector-design]

# Idea

A piece belongs to its original model node. Fine pictures, distant pictures, full colliders and temporary support faces all reference that owner. The spatial index and detail levels are derived from the model. A small resident catalogue preserves the real landing faces while the local full collider is in transit. [District cooker](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/cook-pages.py)

# Method

Traverse the active glTF scene from its roots and compose local transforms along each parent chain. The pair of source file digest and original node index identifies a piece. The manifest records node parents. When a large object crosses 128 m cells, clip its original triangles and keep the same source index on each fragment. Smaller objects stay in one cell with bounds that cover any overhang. A fine placement record references the owner. Embedded and coarse GLB placements store the owner in their node extras. Full collision shapes and the independent catalogue carry the same index. The reader checks these relationships before using them. [Cooked format](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/src/paging/tiles.rs)

For each uncut visual mesh, hash its compressed geometry with neutral materials. The hash covers positions, normals, available vertex colours and UVs, primitive boundaries and indices. When that content serves multiple cells the cooker writes it once and records each source mesh’s ordered material slots separately. Two independent source mesh IDs can share the same geometry. Each fine page lists its resource dependencies and mixes original owned placements with local clipped geometry in source order. An uncut normalized integer colour or UV channel retains its storage type. At a cut, interpolate components along the original triangle and store float32 values. [Geometry cooker](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/cook-pages.py)

At each tick, compute the 3D distance to the actor for collision requests and to the nearer of the actor or camera for visual requests. The default physical scheduler also projects the capsule against page bounds over the estimated service horizon. A full collision page needed for the very next step has highest priority even outside the ordinary distance threshold. [Planning district collision reads](/algorithms/collision-read-planning.md) Each channel allows four reads in flight. The replay models per-request latency and serial transfer against the channel's bandwidth. The deterministic replay reads each file at its modelled deadline. On a paced disk or HTTP replay, workers also decompress and decode separately addressed meshes before delivery. The simulation checks the resulting model and its placement dependencies before publication. The visual lane shares its four transfer slots, bandwidth, jitter and outages between geometry resources and placement pages. Two placements requesting one content hash coalesce into one file read. A verified placement waits for every geometry dependency before replacing the coarse drawing. Evict a page beyond the request radius plus half a cell and release a model after its last resident or requested consumer leaves. [Read queues](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/src/paging/reader.rs)

The physical page stores world-space vertices. A placement with a negative linear determinant reverses an oriented face when its vertices are transformed. The cooker exchanges the second and third indices once for that placement. Two reflected ancestors give a positive determinant and keep the source order. The original mesh can serve any number of owners with different transforms and material keys. The support catalogue classifies the resulting world triangles. A horizontal reflection can keep a walkable top, while a vertical reflection can turn the same source top into an underside. [Collision channel cooker](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/paging_channels.py)

Build the support catalogue from the original oriented triangles of each cooked collision piece. A face qualifies when its normal has a vertical component greater than half its length. Preserve slopes, edges, winding and world positions. The catalogue carries a binding to the ordered coordinates and hashes of the full collision pages. Before each physics step, sweep an enclosing capsule sphere over two fixed steps using its velocity and gravity, with a 1 m margin. Install the corresponding certified triangle meshes in Rapier only where the swept bounds overlap a missing page. An active page has 2 m of extra hysteresis. Replace its temporary colliders when the full file verifies. Both changes occur before advancing the same physics step. [Support catalogue and handoff](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/src/paging/support.rs)


The second support catalogue records a box around each omitted source triangle and combines identical boxes within a single source shape. Each box refers to that shape’s catalogue slot, which resolves its surface ID, owning model node and full parent path. Two door jambs keep separate bounds. The reader checks that the retained and omitted face counts add up to the full page. Before a step it projects the posed capsule, its linear velocity, its angular speed and gravity over that step. Intersection with the bounds of missing geometry gives that full page priority over distance-based demand. The simulation holds the actor at its last pose and preserves its velocity until the page verifies. Camera updates, rendering and content reads continue. A collision page published at the start of a tick can release the hold on that tick. A broad box around a long diagonal triangle can cause an early hold. [Doorway replay](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/check-clearance.py)

The cooker compresses the same bootstrap JSON with zlib level 9 and, when available, Zstandard level 19. It keeps the shorter file, with zlib winning ties. Its content hash covers the selected bytes. The native reader recognises the compression by the digest-named file’s suffix, verifies the encoded length and digest and bounds the decoded size before parsing the catalogue. Both representations use the same directory binding, source owners, face order and collision certificates. Use `--support-storage zlib` to produce input for an older reader. [Cooker](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/paging_support_storage.py) · [Native reader](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/src/paging/support.rs)

For each part and moving box, query the physical page hierarchy with its own next-step swept bounds. Touching a face counts as overlap. At each matching page the guard still checks the omitted faces and their triangle certificates. Keeping the sweeps separate avoids blocking on empty space between two limbs. For collider installation the wider support sweep queries the same hierarchy. The guard visits its previous active pages too, in tile order, to keep the 2 m retention margin, hand off live colliders and evict supports after departure. [Physical page index](/algorithms/collision-read-planning.md)

# Invariants

A resident full collision page has live Rapier colliders. A rejected file never becomes resident. The solver reports the source node, surface, geometry used, tick, contact point, separation and vertical speeds. The active visual placement can be traced to that same node through the original parent chain. The visible fallback still works independently of physical availability. # Failure modes

A rejected full collision file can retry after one second of replay time while verified supports continue serving the local capsule. A corrupted bootstrap catalogue stops protected simulation before any step. An older support-only catalogue uses the bounds of each full page with any omitted faces. This can hold an actor even inside an opening. Without a catalogue the paced local and HTTP readers hold for the threatened full page, while the deterministic directory replay retains its urgent synchronous read. The guard models static terrain. Moving bodies require their separate dynamics path. A character moving through a hole in the footprint keeps falling. [Failure controls](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/paging_checks.py)

A decoded placement alone cannot publish its fine model. With 600 ms latency, a 1 Mbit/s link and an outage during ticks 38–47, the synthetic façade placement decodes on tick 37 and its geometry on tick 53. The existing coarse instance remains until then. A corrupted model reads real bytes on each retry and stays out of the render list. The district’s coarse selection retains major forms and omits small decoration. A sub-metre detail without an authored coarse proxy can appear only when its fine geometry is ready. [Delayed and corrupt resource controls](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/check-sharing.py)

# Alternatives

All-resident tiles provide a control on the same split triangles. The native single-file Rapier replay measures the original faces. Both paths weld duplicate vertices and correct normals along coplanar internal edges in their static triangle colliders. The explicit `--late-collision unprotected` mode reproduces the earlier missing-bridge failure for comparison. # Base colour textures

The district cooker accepts an embedded PNG or JPEG used as a glTF material’s `baseColorTexture` on untransformed `TEXCOORD_0`. Each original primitive keeps its UVs, vertex colours, material factors, alpha mode, cutoff and sampler. V2 embeds the used bitmaps in each independent GLB. V3 also embeds the bitmaps needed by its shared model bank. In v4, the district index records the encoded length, MIME type and SHA-256 of each distinct bitmap. A material selects its image and sampler separately. A clipped local GLB uses the image’s digest filename as its `images[].uri`. Each fine page lists the union of images needed by its local geometry and its shared model placements. Consequently, two samplers can use the same encoded file and two clipped pieces retain the identity of the same original owner. Other material texture channels and transformed UV sets are outside this profile. [Cooker](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/cook-pages.py) · [Storage and ownership checks](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/check-texture-storage.py)

A reader coalesces concurrent image requests on the visual transfer lane. It checks the encoded bytes, decodes the pixels and completes the material bindings before publishing the fine page. The distant version of an opaque primitive uses its base colour factor while the bitmap is in transit. A textured `MASK` or `BLEND` primitive has no distant draw because an untextured substitute could fill its openings. A different opaque primitive of the same object can still supply a distant draw. The fine visual and collision owners survive even when a model has no eligible distant primitive. A one-byte change in the 126-byte PNG caused one rejected read at tick 2. The GPU had zero textures and the screen exactly matched the opaque distant control. # Per-object visual detail

A v4 placement can offer several meshes under its original scene-tree node. Each candidate names a shared mesh resource, its material variant and a bound on its geometric deviation in local metres. The cooker takes the union of their local bounds. The reader applies the full ancestor transform and scales the deviation by a bound on that transform’s maximum stretch, including shear. The same `VisualDetail::choose` as the standalone viewer projects the conservative error into pixels. It permits a cheaper level at or below one pixel and retains the current coarse level up to two pixels. A candidate must reduce the primary triangle count and carry a finite deviation. This bound applies to surface geometry. Material changes and alpha textures can change colour or coverage independently. V2 and v3 retain their original fixed-detail behaviour. [Projection and hysteresis](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/crates/osage-objects/src/visual_detail.rs) · [Page reader](https://github.com/smichea/osage/blob/main/worlds/ville-paris/src/paging/detail.rs)
